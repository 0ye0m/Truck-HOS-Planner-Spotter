   397→   397→10. DEFAULT SCHEDULING STRATEGY
   398→   398→==================================================
   399→   399→
   400→   400→Implement a deterministic strategy.
   401→   401→
   402→   402→At the beginning of a new driving period:
   403→   403→
   404→   404→- Driver has up to 11 driving hours
   405→   405→- Driver has a 14-hour window
   406→   406→- Driver must take the required 30-minute non-driving break after 8 cumulative driving hours
   407→   407→- Driver must stop driving before the 14-hour window expires
   408→   408→- Driver must stop driving before the 11-hour driving limit
   409→   409→- Driver must stop driving when the remaining 70/8 cycle hours are exhausted
   410→   410→
   411→   411→For long-haul planning, after reaching the legal driving capacity:
   412→   412→
   413→   413→- schedule a 10-hour off-duty/sleeper period
   414→   414→- reset the daily 11-hour driving availability and 14-hour window
   415→   415→
   416→   416→Prefer sleeper berth for overnight rest in generated logs because the supplied assessment specifically expects ELD-style daily logs and the FMCSA guide includes sleeper berth as a duty status.
   417→   417→
   418→   418→==================================================
   419→   419→11. 30-MINUTE BREAK LOGIC
   420→   420→==================================================
   421→   421→
   422→   422→This MUST be implemented correctly.
   423→   423→
   424→   424→Track cumulative driving time since the end of the last qualifying 30-minute break.
   425→   425→
   426→   426→When cumulative driving reaches 8 hours:
   427→   427→
   428→   428→- driving cannot continue until a qualifying consecutive 30-minute non-driving period occurs
   429→   429→
   430→   430→The break may be represented as:
   431→   431→
   432→   432→OFF_DUTY
   433→   433→SLEEPER_BERTH
   434→   434→ON_DUTY_NOT_DRIVING
   435→   435→
   436→   436→Do not split the 30 minutes into separate non-consecutive pieces.
   437→   437→
   438→   438→Reset the driving-break counter after the qualifying 30-minute interruption.
   439→   439→
   440→   440→Example:
   441→   441→
   442→   442→Drive 4h
   443→   443→Drive 4h
   444→   444→=> 8h cumulative driving
   445→   445→
   446→   446→Must insert 30-minute break.
   447→   447→
   448→   448→Then:
   449→   449→
   450→   450→Drive remaining available time.
   451→   451→

   638→   638→
   639→   639→The renderer should:
   640→   640→
   641→   641→1. Load the blank log template
   642→   642→2. Fill the appropriate header fields
   643→   643→3. Draw the duty-status lines onto the 24-hour graph
   644→   644→4. Fill total hours
   645→   645→5. Fill miles
   646→   646→6. Fill remarks
   647→   647→7. Fill trip/shipping information when available
   648→   648→8. Save/render a high-resolution result
   649→   649→9. Return it to the frontend
   650→   650→
   651→   651→Use coordinates relative to the template dimensions, not random pixel values scattered throughout the code.
   652→   652→
   653→   653→Create a centralized configuration:
   654→   654→
   655→   655→LOG_TEMPLATE_WIDTH
   656→   656→LOG_TEMPLATE_HEIGHT
   657→   657→GRID_X
   658→   658→GRID_Y
   659→   659→GRID_WIDTH
   660→   660→GRID_HEIGHT
   661→   661→ROW_HEIGHT
   662→   662→HOUR_WIDTH
   663→   663→
   664→   664→Then calculate positions mathematically.
   665→   665→
   666→   666→Do NOT hardcode every hour's x-coordinate manually.
   667→   667→
   668→   668→==================================================
   669→   669→19. GRAPH DRAWING ALGORITHM
   670→   670→==================================================
   671→   671→
   672→   672→The graph has 24 hours.
   673→   673→
   674→   674→Map:
   675→   675→
   676→   676→00:00 -> x0
   677→   677→01:00 -> x0 + hourWidth
   678→   678→02:00 -> x0 + 2*hourWidth
   679→   679→...
   680→   680→23:00 -> x0 + 23*hourWidth
   681→   681→24:00 -> x0 + 24*hourWidth
   682→   682→
   683→   683→Map each duty status to its official row.
   684→   684→
   685→   685→For each activity:
   686→   686→
   687→   687→start_x = time_to_x(activity.start)
   688→   688→end_x = time_to_x(activity.end)
   689→   689→
   690→   690→Draw a horizontal line from start_x to end_x in the correct status row.
   691→   691→
   692→   692→When status changes, draw the appropriate vertical transition.
   693→   693→
   694→   694→The result should visually resemble a manually completed paper ELD graph.
   695→   695→
   696→   696→Do not just draw disconnected horizontal bars.
   697→   697→
   698→   698→==================================================
   699→   699→20. TIME ZONE
   700→   700→==================================================
   701→   701→
   702→   702→The supplied FMCSA guide states that drivers use the time zone in effect at the home terminal for the 24-hour period.
   703→   703→
   704→   704→Therefore:
   705→   705→
   706→   706→- choose a home-terminal time zone based on the provided/derived starting location
   707→   707→- preferably allow an explicit timezone in advanced settings
   708→   708→- use one consistent timezone throughout the daily logs
   709→   709→- crossing state/time-zone boundaries must not arbitrarily change the log's time base
   710→   710→
   711→   711→Document this behavior.
   712→   712→

   737→   737→==================================================

   747→   747→
   748→   748→Use minute-level precision internally.
   749→   749→
   750→   750→Display hours/minutes or decimal hours consistently.
   751→   751→
   752→   752→Example:
   753→   753→

   764→   764→
   765→   765→sum(status_hours) != 24h
