   538→   538→14. HOS RULES
   539→   539→============================================================
   540→   540→
   541→   541→Model the assessment's specified property-carrying driver scenario:
   542→   542→
   543→   543→- 70-hour / 8-day cycle
   544→   544→- 11-hour driving limit
   545→   545→- 14-hour window
   546→   546→- 30-minute break after 8 cumulative driving hours
   547→   547→- 10 consecutive hours off duty for the modeled daily reset
   548→   548→- 34-hour restart when the modeled cycle is exhausted
   549→   549→- pickup = 60 minutes on duty
   550→   550→- dropoff = 60 minutes on duty
   551→   551→- fuel stop = 30 minutes on duty
   552→   552→- fuel planning at least every 1,000 route miles
   553→   553→- no sleeper split in this version
   554→   554→- no adverse-driving exception
   555→   555→- no team driving
   556→   556→- no short-haul exception
   557→   557→

   581→   581→15. HOS SIMULATION STRATEGY
   582→   582→============================================================
   583→   583→
   584→   584→Implement the schedule as a simulation.
   585→   585→
   586→   586→Track state such as:
   587→   587→
   588→   588→- elapsed time
   589→   589→- driving time in current window
   590→   590→- duty time/window
   591→   591→- cumulative driving since break
   592→   592→- cycle hours used
   593→   593→- miles since fuel
   594→   594→- current route position
   595→   595→- current trip leg
   596→   596→- current status
   597→   597→
   598→   598→At every transition, determine the next legal event.
   599→   599→
   600→   600→DO NOT simulate minute-by-minute unless there is a compelling reason.
   601→   601→
   602→   602→Use event-based simulation.
   603→   603→
   604→   604→Calculate how much driving can safely occur before the next constraint.
   605→   605→
   606→   606→Possible next boundaries:
   607→   607→
   608→   608→- destination
   609→   609→- pickup
   610→   610→- dropoff
   611→   611→- fuel threshold
   612→   612→- 8-hour break threshold
   613→   613→- 11-hour driving threshold
   614→   614→- 14-hour window expiry
   615→   615→- cycle exhaustion
   616→   616→
   617→   617→Advance directly to the next boundary.
   618→   618→
   619→   619→This makes the engine efficient and deterministic.
   620→   620→

   643→   643→17. 30-MINUTE BREAK LOGIC
   644→   644→============================================================
   645→   645→
   646→   646→Implement carefully.
   647→   647→
   648→   648→A qualifying non-driving period of at least 30 minutes can satisfy the modeled
   649→   649→break requirement.
   650→   650→
   651→   651→Pickup, fuel, or another qualifying non-driving event can therefore satisfy
   652→   652→the break requirement if the event duration qualifies.
   653→   653→
   654→   654→Do not incorrectly insert another 30-minute break immediately afterward.
   655→   655→
   656→   656→Maintain an explicit break accumulator.
   657→   657→
   658→   658→Two separate 15-minute stops must NOT automatically be treated as one
   659→   659→30-minute qualifying break.
   660→   660→
   661→   661→Write tests for this.
