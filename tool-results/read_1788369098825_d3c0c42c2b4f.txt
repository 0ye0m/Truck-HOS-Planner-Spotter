   397→10. DEFAULT SCHEDULING STRATEGY
   398→==================================================
   399→
   400→Implement a deterministic strategy.
   401→
   402→At the beginning of a new driving period:
   403→
   404→- Driver has up to 11 driving hours
   405→- Driver has a 14-hour window
   406→- Driver must take the required 30-minute non-driving break after 8 cumulative driving hours
   407→- Driver must stop driving before the 14-hour window expires
   408→- Driver must stop driving before the 11-hour driving limit
   409→- Driver must stop driving when the remaining 70/8 cycle hours are exhausted
   410→
   411→For long-haul planning, after reaching the legal driving capacity:
   412→
   413→- schedule a 10-hour off-duty/sleeper period
   414→- reset the daily 11-hour driving availability and 14-hour window
   415→
   416→Prefer sleeper berth for overnight rest in generated logs because the supplied assessment specifically expects ELD-style daily logs and the FMCSA guide includes sleeper berth as a duty status.
   417→
   418→==================================================
   419→11. 30-MINUTE BREAK LOGIC
   420→==================================================
   421→
   422→This MUST be implemented correctly.
   423→
   424→Track cumulative driving time since the end of the last qualifying 30-minute break.
   425→
   426→When cumulative driving reaches 8 hours:
   427→
   428→- driving cannot continue until a qualifying consecutive 30-minute non-driving period occurs
   429→
   430→The break may be represented as:
   431→
   432→OFF_DUTY
   433→SLEEPER_BERTH
   434→ON_DUTY_NOT_DRIVING
   435→
   436→Do not split the 30 minutes into separate non-consecutive pieces.
   437→
   438→Reset the driving-break counter after the qualifying 30-minute interruption.
   439→
   440→Example:
   441→
   442→Drive 4h
   443→Drive 4h
   444→=> 8h cumulative driving
   445→
   446→Must insert 30-minute break.
   447→
   448→Then:
   449→
   450→Drive remaining available time.
   451→
