   538→14. HOS RULES
   539→============================================================
   540→
   541→Model the assessment's specified property-carrying driver scenario:
   542→
   543→- 70-hour / 8-day cycle
   544→- 11-hour driving limit
   545→- 14-hour window
   546→- 30-minute break after 8 cumulative driving hours
   547→- 10 consecutive hours off duty for the modeled daily reset
   548→- 34-hour restart when the modeled cycle is exhausted
   549→- pickup = 60 minutes on duty
   550→- dropoff = 60 minutes on duty
   551→- fuel stop = 30 minutes on duty
   552→- fuel planning at least every 1,000 route miles
   553→- no sleeper split in this version
   554→- no adverse-driving exception
   555→- no team driving
   556→- no short-haul exception
   557→
   558→Keep these assumptions centralized.
   559→
   560→DO NOT duplicate constants across multiple files.
   561→
   562→Example:

   564→HOS_RULES = {

   567→    break_threshold_minutes: 480,

   580→============================================================
   581→15. HOS SIMULATION STRATEGY
   582→============================================================
   583→
   584→Implement the schedule as a simulation.
   585→
   586→Track state such as:
   587→
   588→- elapsed time
   589→- driving time in current window
   590→- duty time/window
   591→- cumulative driving since break
   592→- cycle hours used
   593→- miles since fuel
   594→- current route position
   595→- current trip leg
   596→- current status
   597→
   598→At every transition, determine the next legal event.
   599→
   600→DO NOT simulate minute-by-minute unless there is a compelling reason.
   601→
   602→Use event-based simulation.
   603→
   604→Calculate how much driving can safely occur before the next constraint.
   605→
   606→Possible next boundaries:
   607→
   608→- destination
   609→- pickup
   610→- dropoff
   611→- fuel threshold
   612→- 8-hour break threshold
   613→- 11-hour driving threshold
   614→- 14-hour window expiry
   615→- cycle exhaustion
   616→
   617→Advance directly to the next boundary.
   618→
   619→This makes the engine efficient and deterministic.
